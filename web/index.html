Common questions

How does it work?

Give the agent your website. It learns your offer, finds likely buyers, writes personal emails, follows up and sends your booking link to interested leads.

Will it work for my business?

If you sell to other businesses, yes. Run the free preview above to see who it would contact.

Will emails land in spam?

Emails send from pre-warmed mailboxes with SPF, DKIM and DMARC, not from your own domain. Mailboxes with high bounces are paused.

Where do leads come from?

From a verified business database. You can also upload your own CSV.

How much does it cost?

Finding leads is free. You pay about $0.04 per email sent, within a daily budget you control.

Which countries and languages are supported?

Any country, with emails written in the lead's language where supported.
